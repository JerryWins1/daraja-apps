What is Daraja?

Daraja means “bridge” in Swahili. Daraja Studio is Jerry’s small business. It makes simple apps for families, churches and small businesses — things like a weekly supper planner, a church volunteer schedule, a life-story book for grandparents, and a calm helper for email.

The apps open in your phone’s web browser — nothing to install. The profits help orphans and students in Kenya.

How it works

  • After you join, your own test page opens right away.
  • For each app: watch a short video, follow simple steps, then do a small challenge on your own.
  • Do as many apps as you like, whenever you like. Your place is saved on your phone.
  • You are not being tested — the apps are. If something is confusing, that is exactly what Jerry needs to hear.
  • It costs nothing. As a thank-you, Founding Testers get their first year of the Family & Later-life membership free, then half price for life.
