Privacy

Daraja apps · last updated 26 November 2026

The short version: the Daraja apps keep your information on your own phone. There are no accounts and nothing to sign in to. The only time anything leaves your phone is when you choose to send it.

What stays on your phone

Whatever you put into an app — your lists, your dates, your money, your notes — is stored on the device you put it on. It is not uploaded, not backed up to us, and not visible to anyone else. If you clear the app, or clear your browser's data, it is gone, and we have no copy to give you back.

When you tap the ✨ AI button

Asking the built-in AI is the one thing that sends something off your phone. When you tap it, the app shows you the exact words before anything goes, and nothing is sent until you press Send it.

When you do, this is sent to Daraja's own AI service:

  • your question, and whatever text from the screen the app shows you in that box
  • which app you were in
  • a file or photo, only if you picked one
  • your tester code, if you are a tester and have one

That service passes your question to Anthropic, the makers of Claude, who generate the answer and send it back. Their handling of it is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.

Daraja does not keep your questions or the answers. The only thing kept is a count of how many times each internet connection has asked that day, so that one person cannot run the bill up. That count is held against your network address for two days and then deleted. It is not linked to you by name.

The invisible security check

Every request to that AI service carries an invisible check from Cloudflare Turnstile, which confirms the request came from a real browser on a real Daraja page rather than from a script. You will not see it and there is nothing to tick or solve. It exists because without it anyone on the internet could use the service and run up the cost.

To do this, Cloudflare receives information about your visit. Cloudflare's handling of that information is governed by the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum.

If you use your own AI instead

Some apps offer to hand your question to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot or Meta AI instead. If you choose that, the app opens that company's own site or app and you send it yourself. What happens then is between you and them, under their policy, not ours.

Hosting

These pages are served by GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub's servers see the ordinary details of a request — the page asked for, the time, the network address, the kind of browser.

Children

These apps are not aimed at children and do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes with it.
