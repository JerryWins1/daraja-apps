# Walk-through of every app, 28 Nov 2026

Run by Daraja · tester pack on Jerry's ask (relayed by the Family Money chat). The walker is `tools/walk/walk_all.js` (same idea as `familydash/test/walk.js`, with no per-app script): it starts on each app's real first page, taps only what a person can see and what no pop-up covers, takes a screenshot at every tap, and flags what a person would notice.

## What it covered
- **46 apps** (every app with the house layer v88), each on a **phone** (iPhone 13 size) and an **iPad** (landscape), up to 16 taps each.
- Pop-ups the app puts up are dismissed the way a person would (skip the tour, no thanks, later).

## What it found
- **Page errors: none**, in any app, on either size.
- **Missing files: none** (no 404 on any file an app loads from this site).
- **Sideways scrolling: none.** **"undefined" or "NaN" on screen: none.**
- **Screens it called nearly empty: two, both fine** (a video page, and a link to another page that this offline run blocks).
- **One picture that did not load:** start (tap "No picture" changed nothing on screen).
- **Taps that seemed to change nothing:** 29 flagged. I read 8 of them side by side and every one was not a fault: a slow fade (the tour closing), a video control, a file picker that opens outside the page, a button that asks first through a pop-up, or a link this offline run blocks. The other 21 I did not read, so they are unchecked, not cleared.

## Not covered, and why
- Each walk is a crawl of the buttons on the screen, not a script of the app's main path. The per-app scripts of main-path taps (as in `familydash/test/walk_jerry.json`) are not written yet: that needs someone who knows each app's main path, and it is the right next step for the apps that matter most.
- I read the screenshots for The Key and My Circle in full and the 8 flagged pairs above. The other apps were checked by the automatic flags only. The screenshots are not kept in the repo.
- Run with outside websites blocked, so videos, the AI helper and sending a message are not exercised.
- Audio and the microphone cannot be tried from here.
