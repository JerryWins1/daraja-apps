# Walk-through of every app, 28 Nov 2026

Run by Daraja · tester pack on Jerry's ask (relayed by the Family Money chat). The walker is  (same idea as , with no per-app script): it starts on each app's real first page, taps only what a person can see and what is not covered by a pop-up, takes a screenshot at every tap, and flags what a person would notice.

## What it covered
- **46 apps** (every app with the house layer v88), each on a **phone** (iPhone 13 size) and an **iPad** (landscape), up to 16 taps each.
- Pop-ups the app puts up are dismissed the way a person would (skip the tour, no thanks, later).

## What it found
- **Page errors: none**, in any app, on either size.
- **Missing files: none** (no 404 on any file an app loads from this site).
- **Sideways scrolling: none.** **"undefined", "NaN" or an empty screen: none that held up** (the two it flagged were a video page and a link to another page; see below).
- **One broken picture:** start (tap "No picture" changed nothing on screen).
- **Taps that seemed to change nothing:** 29 flagged, and on reading the screenshots every one I checked was not a fault: a slow fade (the tour closing), a video control, a file picker that opens outside the page, a button that asks first through a pop-up, or a link to another page that this offline run blocks. I read 8 of the 29 side by side and did not read the rest, so those are unchecked, not cleared.

## Not covered, and why
- Each walk is a crawl of the buttons on the screen, not a script of the app's main path. The per-app scripts of main-path taps (as in ) are not written yet: that needs someone who knows each app's main path, and it is the right next step for the apps that matter most.
- Screenshots were read for 4 apps in full (My Circle, The Key, and the 8 flagged pairs above). The other apps were checked by the automatic flags only. The screenshots are not kept in the repo.
- Run with outside websites blocked, so videos, the AI helper and sending a message are not exercised.
- Audio and the microphone cannot be tried from here.
