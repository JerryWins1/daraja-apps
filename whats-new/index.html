Everything that changed in the apps you’re testing, newest first.

You three are the reason any of this gets better. Nearly every line below started as somebody saying “this bit is confusing” — so please keep saying it.

Not seeing the new version?

Close the app completely — swipe it away, don’t just go back — then open it twice. Every app has its version and date at the very bottom, so you can always check what you’ve got.

Thursday 10 September

A new name, a simpler store, and a shopping list for the shoebox drive

Test Day: a page that walks you through the apps one at a time and opens each one in here Feedback Studio

Have a look: open Feedback Studio — close it completely and open it twice if it looks the same.

My Productive Partner is now One Thing One Thing

The old name sounded like an office. The app already said the right words on its own screen — “Now — just this one” — so we used those.

Have a look: if you added it to your Home Screen, the icon underneath still said Now & Next — a name we stopped using weeks ago. Add it again and it should now say One Thing. Your tasks are all exactly where you left them.

The shop has five shelves instead of seven Store

It was hard to tell what to look at. Now each shelf answers one question — is this me? Your household · Later life · Your church · Your business or nonprofit · Free.

Have a look: can you find the app you’d want in under ten seconds? If not, that shelf is named wrong and we want to know.

“What do we still need to buy?” OCC Assistant

For a group filling shoeboxes: one button now writes the whole shopping list — shortest supply first — and hands it to your own Messages or Mail. It also shows the drop-off week and what the shipping donations add up to.

Worth knowing even if you don’t test it: Samaritan’s Purse no longer allow soap or toothpaste in a shoebox. They were staples for years.

Two faults fixed in the apps that hold private things The Key

The Key and The Key · Church Edition were saving into the same place, so on a phone that had opened both, one could overwrite the other. They’re separate now, and nothing was deleted.

“Start fresh — erase my data” was also looking for the wrong thing in most apps, so it often erased nothing while saying it had. It now names the exact record it will clear before you confirm.

Wednesday 9 September

Small screens, and a microphone in every typing box

The squashed screens on a small iPhone Feedback Studio

On an iPhone 12 mini the Feedback Studio was laid out for a desktop, so the title, the instructions and the address box all printed on top of each other — and because you review the other apps through that window, they all looked broken too.

Have a look: open Feedback Studio on your phone and load an app into it. Nothing should overlap and nothing should slide sideways.

You can talk into any box now All apps

Tap into any typing box and a round microphone appears at the end of it. Say your words instead of thumbing them in. Tap it again to stop.

Have a look: Receipt Maker is the quickest place to try it.

One help button instead of two floating ones All apps

The ? and the 💡 used to sit in the middle of the right-hand edge, on top of the words. They’re now one button in the bottom corner that opens the rest.

A date bug that put things on the wrong day Several apps

Anything you did after about 6pm could be recorded as tomorrow — including the daily check-in in My Safety Plan, and its streak.

Have a look: do a check-in this evening and confirm it says today.
