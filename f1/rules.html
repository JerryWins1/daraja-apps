1The league

Five players. Each drafts four real drivers for the season and runs two of them every race weekend. Points come from where those drivers qualify and finish. Most points at the end of the season wins, and the trophy gets engraved.

  • The commissioner runs it: calls the season, runs the draft, sets the deadlines, scores the weekends, posts standings, and settles arguments. In the F1 Group that has been Steve since the start.
  • Everything used to happen in one group text. Picks, results, the draft, the trash talk. Pit Wall keeps the trash talk and takes over the bookkeeping.
  • Decisions are made by vote in the group. When a rule below says "house rule", it is the setting the app applies unless the commissioner changes it in Setup.

2The shape of a season

FebruaryThe commissioner asks "everyone in?", proposes a draft date, and posts the driver allowance for the year.
Draft daySnake order is drawn and posted. Everyone picks four drivers in turn. Rosters are set for the whole season.
Every race weekendPick two of your four before the deadline, or play a wild card. Results score themselves. Standings and usage update.
DecemberFinal standings. Champion crowned. Everyone argues about next year's allowance.
The numbers for 202624 races on the calendar (22 after two cancellations) · 4 drivers per player · 12 drives per driver · 2 wild cards each · 2 drivers run per race.

The allowance is set each year so the math works over the calendar. With 22 weekends and 2 wild cards, a player needs 40 drives; four drivers at 12 each give 48, so there are 8 spare. In 2025 it was 10 per driver, with no slack at all.

3The draft

Once a year, a couple of weeks before the first race. A full redraft every season: there are no keepers, and team changes between seasons do not matter. Drivers are drafted fresh from that year's entry list.

How it runs

  1. Who is in. The commissioner confirms the players for the season. New players are added first.
  2. The order is drawn. Round 1 is random. It is a snake: round 2 reverses round 1, round 3 repeats round 1, round 4 reverses again. Four rounds, twenty picks, everyone ends up with four drivers. The order is posted before draft day.
  3. "Ready." When everyone is ready, the first name is called: "Ryan, you're on the clock."
  4. One driver per turn. Picking out of turn or picking a driver who is already taken gets corrected on the spot. In the app it simply is not allowed.
  5. The driver list is posted with every player's four drivers at full allowance, for example hamilton 12/12. That list is the master record for the season.

Why a snake? In 2020 the order was drawn fresh every round and one player drew last, last, and second to last. By August the group agreed "the need for the snake draft has become abundantly clear," and it has been the rule since 2021.

In Pit WallThe Draft room (Home banner, or Setup → Draft room) lets the commissioner tick who is in, draw the order with one tap, and copy it for the group text. On draft day the board shows every round and who is on the clock; available drivers are listed best to worst by last season's points. The player on the clock taps a driver; the commissioner can pick for anyone and undo the last pick. The final pick writes the rosters and offers the finished driver list to copy.

4Race weekend

The pick

Choose two of your four drivers. That lineup counts for the whole weekend: qualifying, the sprint if there is one, and the race. You can change your mind until the deadline; the last submission counts. Or play a wild card instead (see Driver usage).

The deadline

House rulePicks are due 11:59 pm Friday, Central time, or when qualifying starts if that is earlier. Saturday-race weekends therefore close Thursday night, and sprint weekends close before sprint qualifying on Friday afternoon.

The real rule underneath has always been "before qualifying starts", and that is how odd weekends have been handled: 9:59 pm for a 10 pm sprint qualifying in China, "before 3:30 pm Central" for Miami. The commissioner can switch the deadline style in Setup (Friday night, race-eve, or qualifying start).

After the race

Three things get posted: the weekend's points for each player ranked 1 to 5 with the fastest-lap note, the overall standings with the next race and deadline, and the updated driver usage list.

In Pit WallHome shows the next race, the sessions, the live countdown, your four drivers with drives left, and everyone's submitted lineups once the deadline passes. Results arrive on their own from the public results feed a few hours after the race. Standings shows every weekend's breakdown, and one tap copies a post in the commissioner's own format.

5Scoring

Each of your two drivers earns points in every session of the weekend. Your weekend score is the sum. Tables below are the defaults; the commissioner can change any number in Setup → Scoring.

Race: 20 down to 1P1 = 20, P2 = 19 … P20 = 1. Places beyond the table score zero. Did not finish, did not start, or disqualified = 0 for the race.
Qualifying: by grid row, 10 down to 1Row 1 (P1, P2) = 10, row 2 (P3, P4) = 9 … row 10 (P19, P20) = 1. Both cars on a row score the same.
Sprint: same row tableOn sprint weekends the sprint finishing order is scored by row, 10 down to 1, exactly like qualifying.
Fastest lap: +5If the race's fastest lap belongs to a driver in your lineup that weekend, you get five bonus points.

A driver who is disqualified after the race keeps the qualifying points but scores zero for the race (Russell, Belgium 2024: row 3 for 8 points, race 0). A driver classified a lap down still scores their finishing position. A driver who retires scores zero for the race even if the official results list a position.

House rule · the 22-car gridThe default race table has 20 entries, so P21 and P22 score zero and row 11 scores zero in qualifying. The commissioner can add two more numbers to each table in Setup to change that.
House rule · tiesPlayers on equal points share the higher place in the weekend ranking. A championship tie has never happened; the suggested tiebreak is most weekend wins, then most fastest laps, then a coin flip the commissioner films.

6Driver usage and wild cards

You cannot run your best driver every week. Each driver has a fixed number of drives for the season (12 in 2026). Every weekend you run a driver uses one. Running out is on you.

The usage list is the heart of the league. After each race every player's four drivers are reposted with drives remaining, for example russell 6/12. If you run out of drivers with drives left, you cannot field a full lineup that weekend.

Wild cards

  • Two per season (2 in 2025 and 2026; the number is a setting).
  • Declare one instead of two names, before the deadline. All four of your drivers score that weekend, in every session.
  • A wild card weekend does not use any driver's drives.
  • Use them any time. A wild card on a sprint weekend scores four drivers in three sessions, which is how Robbi posted 153 at Miami 2026.

Wild cards replaced the old rule of "everyone runs all four drivers in the final races." That rule is gone; in 2025 one player assumed it still applied and ran out of drivers.

Substitute drivers

If your driver misses a race, the team's replacement scores for you and the drive counts against your original driver (Bearman for Sainz, 2024; Russell for Hamilton, 2020). A full-season seat change works the same way without a redraft: keep the original driver on the roster, and the replacement's results count for that seat.

In Pit WallUsage shows every player's drivers with drives left and wild cards in hand, and marks the picks that already count for this weekend. Drivers with no drives left cannot be tapped on Home. The wild card button switches off when you have none left. A "Starting point" lets a league join mid-season by typing in everyone's totals and drives used as they stand today.

7Auto-draft: missing the deadline

Miss the deadline and the league picks for you. The stated rule since 2021: "auto draft of top 2 drivers in points for season up to that point."

House rule (the app's default)Auto-draft takes your two highest-scoring drivers by championship points to date that still have drives remaining. It uses drives like any other pick, and it is posted to the group like any other pick.

Why the extra clause: the hand-run version was applied two ways over the years (top two by points, and once a bottom driver "to make sure you had enough drivers for the rest of the season"), which produced a dispute in late 2025 when one player's usage list showed 8 drives left while everyone else had 4. The app's version is the stated rule with the one guard that keeps drives from going negative, applied the same way every time.

A pick that was sent but did not arrive (a messaging mix-up in May 2026) was honored once screenshots surfaced. In the app, the commissioner can enter or fix a pick for anyone from Home, before or after the deadline.

8Special cases the group has already ruled on

  • Wrong weekend, right picks. If picks were logged against the wrong race, the weekend is rescored.
  • Arithmetic slips. Corrections are posted as a full re-post of the standings, not a patch. In the app, the commissioner adjusts a score in Standings and the change shows its work.
  • Saying it twice. Submitting your drivers twice does not double your points.
  • Season cut short. In 2020 the group voted to keep the drafted teams and improvise. The vote is the mechanism.
  • Canceled races drop off the calendar; the allowance is not recalculated mid-season unless the group votes to.
  • Unused wild cards at the end of the season are lost, not applied automatically (no precedent either way; this is the app's default).
  • Ideas raised and parked: handicapping the bottom of the table, a first-half/second-half redraft, 20-for-last sprint scoring, +5 for the sprint fastest lap. Any of these can become a setting if the group votes it in.

How the format got here

  • Before 2020: three drivers each, keepers with a two-draft limit, a redraft after every ten races, two all-three-drivers weekends, 4 points for fastest lap.
  • 2020: the season was cut short. Restarted in July with "top two drivers every week"; usage limits were bolted on mid-season. Everyone agreed the snake draft was a requirement going forward.
  • 2021: the modern format. Four drivers, snake draft, 11 drives each, all four in the last race, auto-draft of the top two.
  • 2024: a founding member retires, a new player joins. 24 races, 11 drives, last two races all four.
  • 2025: wild cards replace the all-four finale. Sprints scored by row. The scoring formula written out in full for the first time.
  • 2026: 12 drives plus 2 wild cards, a 22-car grid, and Pit Wall.
