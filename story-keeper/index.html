In My Own Words

Your life, in your own words — for the people you love.

Let's begin 🕊

There's no typing here. Pick a chapter of your life, and I'll ask you a question. You press the red button and just talk — like telling a story at the kitchen table. Your words are saved exactly as you say them.

🎤 Before you start: sit in a quiet room, close to the microphone — arm's reach or less. A phone held nearby or a headset with a mic makes your words come out right the first time.

🎁 Making this for someone you love? The best sessions are two chairs and one phone: you ask, they talk, the book remembers. Sunday afternoons were made for this.
