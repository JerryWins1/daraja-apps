🛡 Selling safely — read once
· Meet in a busy public place, in daylight — many police stations have "safe exchange zones."
· Cash or instant transfer you confirm on YOUR phone. Never checks, never "I'll pay the rest later."
· Bring a friend for big items; let someone know where you're going.
· Porch pickup with payment first works great for small stuff.
· If a buyer feels off, they are. There's always another buyer.
