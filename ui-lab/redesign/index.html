One card in your hands — the day is a deck. Only the NOW card exists on screen, huge and calm; the next cards peek from beneath. Swipe up = done and the next card rises; swipe down = later; hold = park the thought. "Peek at my day" fans the deck out. ✓ The most focused screen ever made for a racing brain — literally nothing else to look at. Feels like a game, not a chore list. ✗ No overview unless you ask for one; planning happens in a separate peek view.

The buddy IS the app — your day as a conversation. Sparkle talks, you answer with big buttons (or the mic). "Next is breakfast — want to start?" ▶ Start · 📋 show my day · 🧠 park a thought. Celebrations, nudges, and family notes all arrive as messages. ✓ Everyone already knows how to use a chat. The Warm Doorway isn't a feature here — it's the whole interface. Zero learning curve, maximum kindness. ✗ Slower for power users; lists and planning live behind a message instead of on a screen.

My vote this round: A is still the best skeleton — but B and C aren't competitors, they're A's two best organs. The Deck is what A's focus mode should become (press Start and the river fades away, leaving one card in your hands). The Buddy is how kids and first-timers should meet the app (chat mode as the front face for young profiles, river underneath). One app: the River for the day, the Deck when you're working, the Buddy voice all the way through. Say "build the river," or pick any piece.
