The Key · Church Edition

One page that tells the NEXT trustee, treasurer, or pastor where everything lives — so nothing the church needs is locked in one person's head. Ten minutes, once. Tape it inside the office cabinet.

🏢 The building

Shutoffs, panels, alarm procedure, where the keys are — the stuff only one person usually knows.

💻 Accounts & services

Bank, giving platform, website, utilities, email — and WHERE the password list is kept (never the passwords themselves).

📄 Papers & policies

Deed, insurance policies, van title, bylaws, 501(c)(3) letter, service contracts — drawer, safe, or lawyer.

🤝 The people to call

Plumber who knows the building, electrician, insurance agent, payroll service, the member who fixes the furnace.

💌 Anything else they should know

A wish, an instruction, a kindness. In your own words.
🔒 A note on safety: never write actual passwords on this page — write where they're kept. This page saves only on your own device, and prints from it. Nothing is sent anywhere.
