🎛 HQ Kit

Eight quick questions. Then you get a control room built from your own answers, like Daraja HQ.

First name is fine. It shows at the top: "Jerry's HQ".

One or two sentences. This becomes the first line of your HQ and the context Claude gets.

This becomes your Focus: Claude turns it into ordered steps with where, why and how.

They become your People list, where their messages and feedback collect.

HQ does not replace it on day one; it links to it and pulls the next step out of it.

One per line, date first if you know it. HQ shows them under Today.

Each line becomes a tap-to-copy chip in your HQ.

Steward, Daraja HQ and the others all follow the same rule: one weekly note, and a tap on the shoulder only when it matters.
