🎛 HQ Kit

Nine quick questions. Then you get a control room built from your own answers, like Daraja HQ.

First name is fine. It shows at the top: "Jerry's HQ".

One or two sentences. This becomes the first line of your HQ and the context Claude gets.

Most people have two or three going at once. Each line becomes its own Focus. Add "by" and a date to any goal that has one.

One sentence per goal, same order as above, written as if it already happened. Skip a line if you are not sure yet; the HQ will ask again.

They become your People list, where their messages and feedback collect.

HQ does not replace it on day one; it links to it and pulls the next step out of it.

One per line, date first, any common format. HQ shows them under Today with how far off they are. Goal dates from question 3 land there too.

Each line becomes a tap-to-copy chip in your HQ.

Steward, Daraja HQ and the others all follow the same rule: one weekly note, and a tap on the shoulder only when it matters.
