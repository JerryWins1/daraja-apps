When something is wrong

Too small, wrong words, you don’t know what to tap next, it did something odd. Any of those counts. Four moves:

👆Tap Point, then tap the thing
🎤Tap the red mic and say why
📸Tap Screenshot — it takes the picture for you
🙂Nothing wrong? Say that too

Three or four notes per app is plenty. Short is fine. Grumpy is fine.

Tap ✅ Done at the bottom of the tester. It writes the report for you. Then tap 📨 Send to Claude — it goes straight to Jerry, on a phone or a computer. Nothing to pick, no number needed.
