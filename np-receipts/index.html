🧾 Receipt Maker

Donor acknowledgment letters, done right — one, or a whole January's worth

1 · Your organization (saved on this device)

2 · The gifts

One gift per row. Fill in as many as you need — the ✚ button adds another.
For a gift of things rather than money, start the amount box with in-kind: — for example in-kind: used laptop. A description on its own is not enough, so that a mistyped amount can never turn into an in-kind gift by accident.

One gift per line: Name, amount, date — e.g. Mary Smith, 250, 3/14/2026 (2026-03-14 works too). For an in-kind gift describe it instead of an amount: Bill Jones, in-kind: used office desk, 2026-05-02

Unticked: under each gift, say what they got back (a dinner ticket, an auction item) and what it was worth. The letter works out how much they can deduct. Leave it blank if they got nothing.

✏️ Tap any letter below to change its words. When they look right, tap 🖨 Print them all above.

Everything stays on this device. Not tax advice — when in doubt, ask your accountant.
