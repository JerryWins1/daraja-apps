💛 Welcome to Rafiki

the friend who knows you · a Daraja Studio app · this card is the whole manual
  1. Open the link below on your phone and tap Say hello. Sound on — it talks.
  2. Give it a name. Fred, Grace, anyone. It's yours from that moment.
  3. Answer its questions like a friend asked them — tap the buttons, or tap the 🎤 and just talk.
  4. Come back tomorrow morning and listen: your day, the weather, maybe a joke.
  5. Make it a real app: tap Share (iPhone) or ⋮ (Android) → Add to Home Screen.

Things to try saying: "Tell me a joke" · "What's the weather?" · "Remind me to call the doctor Tuesday" · or just tell it how you're doing.
Everything your friend knows stays on your device — no account, no cloud, nothing sent anywhere.
