# Business Manager — video scripts

Each line is one screen. The time is when it appears. To add your voice: play the video, read each line as its screen comes up, and record with your phone’s voice memo or iMovie. Or just send the videos as they are — every word is already on screen.

## Short overview

- `0:00` **Business Manager** — The office in your truck — for plumbers, HVAC, cleaners, painters, handymen… any one-truck business.
- `0:04` **Your day at a glance** — Who owes you, what got paid this month, and today’s jobs — the moment you open it.
- `0:13` **It tells you what needs you** — Late invoices, estimates to follow up, finished jobs to bill. One tap each.
- `0:22` **Every job, one next step** — Each job moves through simple steps. The big button always shows what’s next.
- `0:31` **Send the estimate** — It writes the text for you. You press send from your own phone.
- `0:38` **The customer signs on their phone** — They look it over, sign with a finger, and tap Approve. The signed copy comes back to you.
- `0:48` **Snap the receipt** — It reads the items. You say which job each one is for — or the shop, or personal.
- `0:57` **Get paid faster** — Every invoice has Pay buttons with the amount filled in. Late ones get a friendly reminder.
- `1:06` **Know your real profit** — Money in, money out, profit per job — and a clean package for your accountant.
- `1:15` **Only what you use** — A two-minute interview sets it up for how you work. Skip estimates or crew and they never show up.
- `1:24` **Try it** — Open the link on your phone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, and it works like an app.

*Length: about 1:30*

## A day in the life

- `0:00` **A day with Business Manager** — Follow Dan, a plumber with one helper, from his first coffee to home by supper.
- `0:04` **Coffee and the Today screen** — Dan sees $175 owed (one late), and a water heater install at 9.
- `0:12` **What needs him** — A late invoice, an estimate with no answer for four days, and a finished job not billed yet.
- `0:22` **A friendly nudge** — One tap writes a polite reminder with the pay link. He sends it from his own phone.
- `0:30` **Follow up on the church job** — Same for the estimate he sent on Monday. Two minutes, and the office work is done.
- `0:39` **Supply run** — At Home Depot he grabs parts for two jobs, a drill bit set, and a candy bar. Tap + → Receipt.
- `0:48` **It reads the receipt** — Store, date, every item, tax and total — read right off the photo.
- `0:56` **Split what’s shared** — The PEX elbows are for two jobs, so he splits them. Most of the rest goes to today’s water heater.
- `1:06` **Shop tools, and his snack** — Drill bits go to the shop. The candy bar is personal — it stays off the books.
- `1:15` **Charge the customer** — One checkbox adds the water heater parts to Sarah’s bill, with his 15% markup.
- `1:23` **Saved — and sorted** — Tax is spread across the items, so each job carries its fair share. The photo is kept for the IRS.
- `1:33` **On my way** — One tap tells Sarah he’s 20 minutes out.
- `1:39` **What Sarah sees** — A normal text from Dan’s own number. No app for her to download.
- `1:46` **Luis clocks in** — His helper’s hours land on this job, so Dan knows what the job really cost.
- `1:54` **Job done — bill it** — The big button says exactly what’s next.
- `2:00` **The invoice builds itself** — From the job’s prices and the parts he bought this morning.
- `2:07` **Sent with a pay link** — Sarah gets the invoice and a way to pay before Dan’s back in the truck.
- `2:16` **Sarah pays from her couch** — PayPal, Zelle, card or check — the buttons have the amount filled in.
- `2:24` **Paid** — Dan records the payment. The job closes itself and offers a thank-you text with a review link.
- `2:32` **Did it make money?** — Charged, minus parts, minus Luis’s time. Dan sees what he kept on every job.
- `2:41` **A new call** — Tom has a leak. Dan adds him and the job in a few taps — or talks into the box.
- `2:49` **Priced from his own list** — Tap items from his price list, or let the AI draft them from his notes.
- `2:57` **Tom signs right away** — He signs with his finger and taps Approve. His phone sends the signed approval back to Dan.
- `3:07` **Signed** — Tom sees a thank-you with his signature. His phone sends the signed approval to Dan.
- `3:15` **Dan gets the approval** — Tom’s text has a link. Dan taps it once…
- `3:21` **…and it’s on file** — Signature, name, time and amount — saved on the job and in Tom’s customer record.
- `3:29` **Book it** — He picks a day for Tom on the job, and it’s on the schedule — and his phone calendar.
- `3:38` **Home by supper** — Paid this month, owed, costs, profit. No paperwork waiting on the kitchen table tonight.
- `3:46` **That’s the whole day** — Office work done in minutes, from the truck.

*Length: about 3:52*

## The complete how-to

- `0:00` **Business Manager: the complete how-to** — Every screen, step by step. About ten minutes — pause any time.

**1 · Getting started**

- `0:04` **Getting started** — Open the link on your phone. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android: ⋮ → Install app.
- `0:08` **The welcome screen** — Set it up for your business, or look around a sample business first. Nothing leaves your phone.
- `0:18` **A two-minute interview** — It asks how you work, then shows only the parts you need. Start with your business and your name.
- `0:28` **Your trade** — Pick your trade. It loads typical prices and wording you can change later.
- `0:35` **How customers reach you** — This goes on your estimates and invoices.
- `0:42` **Do you send estimates?** — Say no and the app skips straight from “new job” to the work.
- `0:49` **Itemized or one price?** — List parts and labor, or just give one price per job. Either way works.
- `0:58` **Deposits, schedule, materials, crew** — A few yes-or-no questions. Add a helper and their hourly pay if you have one.
- `1:07` **How you get paid** — Pick every way customers pay you. Each one becomes a Pay button on your invoices.
- `1:16` **All set** — It shows what it turned on and what it hid. You can change any of it later in More.
- `1:24` **Your Today screen** — Empty for now. The four tabs at the bottom are all you need: Today, Jobs, New, Money, More.

**2 · The Today screen**

- `1:33` **The Today screen** — From here on we’re using a sample plumbing business, so every screen has something in it.
- `1:37` **Money at a glance** — Owed to you (and how much is late), and what’s been paid this month. Tap either one for details.
- `1:46` **Today and the next 7 days** — Today’s jobs first, then the week ahead.
- `1:53` **Needs you** — Your to-do list, built for you: late invoices, quiet estimates, unbilled jobs. Tap one to deal with it.

**3 · Customers & jobs**

- `2:02` **Customers & jobs** — Everything starts with a job.
- `2:06` **The + button** — Add a job, a customer, a receipt or an expense from anywhere in the app.
- `2:14` **New job** — Pick a customer or add a new one right here. Name the job, add notes — tap the 🎤 on your keyboard to talk.
- `2:23` **The job page** — Where it stands, the customer with call, text and map buttons, and one big button for the next step.
- `2:33` **All your jobs** — Search, or filter by step: new, estimate sent, approved, scheduled, working, done, paid.
- `2:42` **Customer records** — Every customer’s jobs, what they’ve paid you, and their signed estimates — in one place.

**4 · Pricing & estimates**

- `2:51` **Pricing & estimates** — Price it, send it, get it signed.
- `2:54` **Add from your price list** — Tap an item, or type your own. New items you type are saved to your list for next time.
- `3:04` **Quantity and price** — Change how many or the price for this job.
- `3:10` **Or let AI draft it** — “Draft it for me” turns your notes into line items. It opens your AI app; copy its answer and paste it back.
- `3:20` **Send the estimate** — It writes the message with a link. Text it, email it, or copy it. You always press send yourself.
- `3:30` **Make it sound better** — Optional: ✨ rewrites the message with your AI. Copy its answer, come back, tap Paste.
- `3:39` **What the customer sees** — Your name, the work, the prices, the total, your terms. No app or login for them.
- `3:48` **They sign with a finger** — Name, signature, a checkbox, and Approve.
- `3:54` **Their approval comes to you** — Their phone texts you the approval with a link. Tap the link…
- `4:02` **…and it’s saved** — The signature, name, time and amount go on the job and in the customer’s record.
- `4:11` **iPhone tip** — If the link opens in Safari instead of your Home Screen app, tap Copy, open the app, and paste it into the job under “They said yes”.
- `4:21` **Signed copy on file** — Open it, print it, or save it as a PDF any time.

**5 · Schedule & updates**

- `4:28` **Schedule & updates** — Pick a day, and keep the customer in the loop.
- `4:32` **Pick a day and time** — The job moves to Scheduled. Add it to your phone calendar, or text the customer a confirmation.
- `4:41` **Your schedule** — Everything coming up, by day. Approved jobs without a date wait at the bottom.
- `4:49` **Send an update** — On my way, running late, work is done — one tap writes the text.

**6 · Receipts & costs**

- `4:57` **Receipts & costs** — Snap it, say where each item goes, done.
- `5:01` **Three ways in** — Take a picture, pick a photo, or paste an emailed receipt from Home Depot, Lowe’s or Amazon.
- `5:10` **It reads the receipt** — Store, date, items, tax and total. Check them — fix anything it misread.
- `5:18` **Most of this is for…** — Pick the job most of it is for. Every item follows.
- `5:25` **…then change the exceptions** — A tool for the shop, supplies for stock, or a snack that’s personal and stays off the books.
- `5:35` **Split an item** — Bought one box for two jobs? Split it by amount, or half and half.
- `5:42` **Charge the customer** — Tick a job to add these parts to its price, with your markup.
- `5:49` **Where it went** — Tax is shared out across the items. Undo it any time to redo it.
- `5:56` **Quick expense** — No receipt to scan? Type the amount and where. It guesses the kind of expense for you.
- `6:05` **Import a bank or card statement** — Download transactions as CSV from your bank and pick the file. It sorts charges and skips ones you already have.
- `6:16` **Check and save** — Business charges are ticked and sorted. Personal ones (like Netflix) and card payments are left out.

**7 · Crew & hours**

- `6:25` **Crew & hours** — For when someone works with you.
- `6:29` **Clock in on a job** — Pick who. Their hours count toward that job’s real cost.
- `6:36` **On the clock** — A green bar on Today shows who’s working where. Tap Clock out when they’re done.
- `6:44` **Hours for payroll** — A week at a time, with pay. Download it for Gusto, ADP or your accountant. The app doesn’t run payroll.

**8 · Invoices & getting paid**

- `6:54` **Invoices & getting paid** — Bill it, send it, get paid.
- `6:58` **Deposits and progress bills** — Big job? Bill a deposit or a progress payment. The final invoice subtracts what was already billed.
- `7:09` **The invoice** — Built from the job. Change the due date if you need to.
- `7:15` **Send with a pay link** — The message includes a link to see and pay the invoice.
- `7:22` **What the customer sees** — Pay buttons for each way you take money, with the amount filled in.
- `7:30` **Record a payment** — Amount, date and how they paid. Paid in full closes the job and offers a thank-you text.
- `7:39` **Late? Send a reminder** — Late invoices show up on Today. One tap sends a polite reminder with the pay link.

**9 · Money & reports**

- `7:48` **Money & reports** — Know where you stand — and hand your accountant a clean package.
- `7:52` **Money in, money out, profit** — Pick the month or year. Sales tax is kept out of income. It suggests what to set aside for taxes.
- `8:02` **Where the money went** — Your costs by kind: materials, fuel, tools, insurance…
- `8:09` **Job by job** — What each job brought in and what you kept.
- `8:15` **For your accountant** — A Profit & Loss to print, plus income, expense and invoice lists, and a QuickBooks upload file.
- `8:24` **Profit & Loss** — One page for the year. Print it or save it as a PDF.

**10 · Settings & safety**

- `8:31` **Settings & safety** — Make it yours, and keep your data safe.
- `8:35` **More** — Business info, how you get paid, price list, crew, messages, and your AI helper.
- `8:42` **Turn features on or off** — Don’t use something? Switch it off and it disappears. Nothing you entered is lost.
- `8:51` **Getting paid** — Add your PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle or a Square/Stripe link. Each one becomes a Pay button.
- `9:01` **Ask for reviews** — Paste your Google review link. It’s added to the thank-you text after a job is paid.
- `9:09` **Back up once a month** — Your data lives on your phone. “Back up” saves a copy to a file. Keep it somewhere safe.
- `9:18` **You’re ready** — Start with one real job today. Questions or ideas? Text whoever sent you this — that’s exactly what helps while we test.

*Length: about 9:24*
