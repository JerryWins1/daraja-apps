The questions everyone asks

It looks old. Where is the new version?

Close the app completely — swipe it away, not just back — then open it once more. The version and date are at the very bottom of every app. If it still says an old date, the phone has kept a copy; tap the ? corner in the app, then Start fresh — only if you are happy to lose what is saved — or ask us.

Where is my information kept? Do you have it?

On your phone, and nowhere else. We never see it. That is also why it does not follow you to a second phone by itself — each app has a Backup button (the ? corner) that saves a file you can move over.

Do I need an account or a password?

No. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to remember. The only code you may meet is a tester code, which just says how long the app stays open for you. Testers type TESTING, or the code that came with their invitation link or from Jerry.

Typing is hard for me.

Tap into any typing box and a 🎤 appears. Tap it, say the words, tap it again. Every Daraja app has this, in every box.

The words are too small.

Tap the Aa in the corner of any app. Three sizes. It remembers.

Something went wrong and I don’t know what.

Reloading fixes almost everything and never loses your saved work. If it keeps happening, use the box below — say what you tapped just before, and we will find it.

How do I put it on my phone like a real app?

iPhone: open the app in Safari, tap the Share ⬆️ button, scroll, tap Add to Home Screen. Android: tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen. It then sits on your front screen with its own icon, and works without signal.

What does it cost, and does it stop working?

One price, paid once. While we are testing, everything is free. No subscription. Owned apps keep working forever; nothing switches off.

I want to start over.

Tap the ? corner button in the app, then Start fresh. It erases that one app’s saved work, and nothing else. It asks twice.

Ask a person

Jerry reads these himself, usually the same day. Say what you were trying to do and what happened.
