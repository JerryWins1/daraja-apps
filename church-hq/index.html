⛪ Church HQ

The one place your church app is kept up to date

What this is

Your church app shows what you type in here. Change a service time on this page, tap Publish, and every phone in the congregation has it — without a website, without a web designer, and without waiting on anybody.

  1. Fill in The basics — name, times, address.
  2. Add your people, ministries and events as you go.
  3. Tap Publish. That is the whole job.

Do I need a website?

No. Plenty of churches have none, and this works perfectly without one — HQ is where your information lives. If you do have a website, keep it; nothing here touches it, and the app simply shows what you type here.

Publish

This sends what you have typed to every phone that has your church app.

The mailbox ONE-TIME SETUP

Publishing needs a free mailbox on your own Google account — five minutes, once, and it is yours forever. No accounts with us, no monthly fee, nothing to cancel.

  1. Open script.google.com → New project.
  2. Delete the sample code, paste ours in (button below), then Save.
  3. Deploy → New deployment → Web app → Execute as Me → Who has access Anyone.
  4. Copy the address it gives you (it ends in /exec) and paste it here.

No mailbox yet? WORKS ANYWAY

You can still publish by hand — copy your church file and send it to whoever looks after your app. Nothing is lost either way.

Who else can edit?

Anyone you give this link and the passphrase to. Keep it to two or three people.
