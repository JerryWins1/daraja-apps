What this is

Daraja Studio is a small family business. We make simple apps for people who don't much like apps — one thing at a time, big buttons, plain words.

Every app you'll see is finished and working. They're free while we're testing. What we need is the one thing we can't make ourselves: an honest opinion from somebody who isn't us.

“Daraja” is Swahili for bridge. What the apps earn goes towards school fees for students in Kenya.

What to do — three steps

  1. Open the store and pick one Choose whichever one sounds useful to you. Don't pick one to be polite — pick the one you'd actually open.
  2. Use it for real, not for show Put your own real things in it. Ten minutes of genuine use tells us more than an hour of clicking about.
  3. Tell us the truth Especially the bad parts. “I didn't know what to do here” is the single most useful thing you can say, and it's never rude.

The easiest way to tell us

There's a little tool that records what you say while you use an app, so you don't have to remember anything or write anything down.

Open Feedback Studio, tap the app you're trying, then just talk as you go — say whatever you're thinking, including “what am I supposed to do here?”. When you're done it writes the whole report for you, and you send it to Jerry in one tap.

Two things worth knowing

Add it to your home screen and it behaves like a real app, with its own icon. On an iPhone: the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

If an app talks and you hear nothing, check the little switch on the left edge of your iPhone — if orange is showing, the phone is silenced and no app can speak.
