Questions a wise trustee asks

Do we have to take the Care Plan?

No. The $149 app is complete on its own and updates itself with our general improvements. The Care Plan adds the watching, the seasonal freshness, and the person who answers — most churches want that, but it's genuinely optional.

What if we cancel after a year?

Then you cancel. The app keeps running exactly as it is, forever, and you can restart care whenever you like. No penalties, no hostage-taking, no hard feelings.

Who updates our content week to week?

Your own staff, from a phone, with the simple update page and key we hand you at setup. The Care Plan is for everything around that — the plumbing, the seasons, the improvements.

Is our congregation's data safe?

There are no member accounts and no database of your people — the app is content out, not data in. There is nothing to breach.

Why so much cheaper than everyone?

We're a family studio, not a venture-funded platform. And profits from every Daraja purchase help students in Kenya stay in school — which is the whole reason we build.
