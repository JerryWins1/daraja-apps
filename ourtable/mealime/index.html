What it does

  • 📅A week of dinners in one tapIt fills the empty nights from the dinners you cook. Change any night with a tap.
  • 🧺One shopping list, by aisleEverything for the week, added up and sorted by the part of the store. Change how many are at the table and the amounts change too.
  • ❤️Family favoritesEach person says ❤️ or 👎 to a dinner — pass the phone around. The week skips the 👎 and picks the most ❤️ first.
  • 👩‍🍳Cook modeOne step at a time, in big letters, and the screen stays on while you cook.
