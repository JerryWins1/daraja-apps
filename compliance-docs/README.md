# Nonprofit Compliance & Paperwork Documentation

This directory contains all critical compliance and regulatory documents for the organization.

## Directory Structure

### `/990-filings/`
- IRS Form 990-N (e-postcard) for small organizations
- IRS Form 990-EZ for organizations with less than $50,000 in gross receipts
- IRS Form 990 for larger organizations
- Related schedules and supporting documentation
- Annual filing receipts and acknowledgment letters

### `/banking-documents/`
- Dormant account claims
- Customer information refresh forms
- Account verification documents
- Banking correspondence
- Wire transfer instructions

### `/donor-records/`
- Donor contribution records
- Schedule B (List of Contributors) - **CONFIDENTIAL**
- Acknowledgment letters
- Donation tracking spreadsheets
- Year-end giving reports

### `/tax-documents/`
- Tax summary letters
- Charitable contribution letters
- Income statements for tax purposes
- Year-end financial summaries
- Tax return copies (if applicable)

### `/secretary-of-state/`
- Annual reports to Secretary of State
- Corporate filing documents
- Business registration renewals
- State compliance certificates

### `/templates/`
- Blank forms and templates for recurring filings
- Checklist templates
- Compliance reminder schedules
- Document templates for officers/board

### `/archive/`
- Previous years' filings (maintain for 7 years minimum)
- Historical records
- Superseded documents

## Important Notes

**Confidentiality & Security:**
- Schedule B (donor list) is SENSITIVE and should be protected
- Keep financial documents secure and access-controlled
- Verify all uploaded documents are current before filing

**Filing Requirements:**
- 990-N (e-postcard) due annually if gross receipts < $50,000
- 990-EZ or 990 due within 5½ months of fiscal year end
- Secretary of State annual reports due per state requirements
- State charitable registration renewals per state schedule

**Document Retention:**
- Keep all compliance documents for minimum 7 years
- Maintain copies of all filed returns indefinitely
- Archive outdated files rather than deleting
