🧭 Daraja Compass your life, mapped by you

What this is for. Daraja Compass helps you work out your life goals — what you actually want the years ahead to hold — and turns that into a plan you can act on. You answer a few plain questions, it draws your path, and every path ends the same way: with one small, dated step you can do this week.
You can type your answers or read them aloud — whichever is easier.
Install on iPhone: tap the Share button ⬆︎, then "Add to Home Screen".
