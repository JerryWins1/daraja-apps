What is Duka?

Duka is an app for a small phone and electronics shop in Kenya. The owner runs the whole shop from one phone:

  • 🛒 Sell: M-Pesa, cash, card or deni, with a receipt that shows the warranty date
  • 📦 Stock: it goes down with every sale and warns when it runs low
  • 💵 Money: the evening cash count, money out, bills and a weekly report
  • 👥 Customers: who owes (deni) and repairs
  • 📣 Offers: WhatsApp or SMS offers, only to customers who said yes

It opens in the browser. There is nothing to install, and it works with no internet. The shop owner's data stays on their phone.

2 · Do the test (about 45 minutes)

The test page takes you through four parts, one at a time:

  1. Watch the explainer (you may have done it above).
  2. Steps: 24 small steps in the example shop. After each one, tap ✅ It worked, ❌ It didn't, or 🤷 Not sure.
  3. Challenge: you are Brian in Kisumu. Set up your own shop and do a few jobs on your own, then tap Check my work.
  4. Your own words: what is Duka for? Then tap Send my results. They go to Jerry.

Already on the Kenya testing team? Use the same phone as before. Your saved place and name are kept.
