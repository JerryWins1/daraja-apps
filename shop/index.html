Questions people ask

Can I really try everything free?

Yes — every "Try it" button opens the real, full app. No sign-up, no card. Prices show what apps will cost when the store opens.

How do I get the app on my phone like a real app?

Open the app in your browser → tap Share (iPhone) or the menu (Android) → Add to Home Screen. It gets its own icon and works offline, just like an app-store app.

Do I need an account? Where does my data go?

No account, ever. Your tasks, stories and lists live on your own device and nowhere else. We couldn't peek if we wanted to.

What about updates? Do I pay again?

Never. When we improve an app, the new version is simply there the next time you open it. Buy once, improved forever.

What phones and computers does it work on?

All of them — iPhone, Android, iPad, and any computer with a browser. Nothing to download from an app store; the link IS the app.

Where does the money go?

Daraja Studio is a family business. Profits help fund scholarships that keep students in Kenya in school — that's the whole reason we build.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Try it free first — that usually answers it. If you bought and it's still not right, reply to your receipt within 30 days for a refund.
