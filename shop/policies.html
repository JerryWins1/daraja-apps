The small print, in big letters

Four short promises. They are the same for every app and every membership.

💵 Free, and donations

Every app is free, for everyone. No card, no account, no subscription, and nothing is ever locked.

If an app helps you, you can give a suggested donation. It is completely optional, and the app is yours whether you give or not. If you get an app free now, it stays free for you, even if the store ever charges for apps later.

Donations go to Loving InDeed, Inc., a 501(c)(3) charity that helps orphans and students in Kenya. You receive nothing in exchange for a gift, so it may be tax-deductible as the law allows; your receipt comes from Loving InDeed. Your card statement will read LOVING INDEED INC.

Gave by mistake, or the wrong amount? Reply to your donation receipt and we will put it right.

🔐 Privacy

There is no account and no login. What you type stays on your phone, in the app, and never comes to us — we could not read it if we wanted to. No ads, no selling of anything.

Here is everything that does reach us, all of it:

  • That an app was opened that day. Once a day, each app sends a count that says which app and which date, and nothing else: no name, no id, no cookie, and nothing you typed. We use it to know which apps people use.
  • Which app a donation came from. When you give through an app, the donation link carries that app's name, so the gift is recorded against the app that raised it. Your email comes with the gift so the donation receipt can reach you.
  • A sign-up, only what you choose to give. If you tap Keep me posted or Tell me when things change, we receive your name and the email or phone number you type, which shelf or app you asked about, and whether you ticked the box for news from Loving InDeed. You can ask to be removed any time — write to the address below and you are off the list.
  • What you send us yourself: a question from Help, or a report while testing.

If you tap ✨ Ask, your question goes to an AI service to be answered — leave out anything private.

♿ Accessibility

Every app is built for eyes and hands that are not twenty any more: text is never under 13px, every button is at least 44px tall, there is an Aa button in every app for larger text, you can talk instead of type in any box, and colours are measured for contrast. Every app prints clean, black on white. If something is hard to read or reach, tell us — it is a fault, and we fix faults.

🔄 Updates and your information

Updates arrive on their own when you open the app — nothing to install. Every update is free, for everyone. What you type is yours: you can always save a copy of your information and print it, and renewing brings everything back.
