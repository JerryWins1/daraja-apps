The small print, in big letters

Four short promises. They are the same for every app on the shelf.

💵 Refunds

Every app is try before you buy — the real app, free, no card. If you buy one and it is not right for you, reply to your receipt within 30 days and we refund it. No form, no reason needed.

🔐 Privacy

There is no account and no login. What you type stays on your phone, in the app, and never comes to us — we could not read it if we wanted to. No ads, no trackers, no selling of anything. The only thing we ever receive is your email if you buy, so we can send the receipt.

♿ Accessibility

Every app is built for eyes and hands that are not twenty any more: text is never under 13px, every button is at least 44px tall, there is an Aa button in every app for larger text, you can talk instead of type in any box, and colours are measured for contrast. Every app prints clean, black on white. If something is hard to read or reach, tell us — it is a fault, and we fix faults.

🔄 Updates and ownership

You buy an app once and it is yours. Updates arrive on their own when you open the app — nothing to install, nothing to renew. If we ever stopped, the app on your phone keeps working.
